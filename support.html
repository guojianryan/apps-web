Common questions

Quick answers

Most questions are answered here. If yours isn't, just email us.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Echo Quillo runs on iPhone and iPad with iOS 26 or later, and on Mac. You can practice English, German and Chinese (Simplified or Traditional).

Audio transcription or translation doesn't start.

Both run on your device using Apple's speech and translation technology. The first time you use a language, your device may need to download it. Stay connected to the internet and try again.

Also check that Echo Quillo is allowed to use speech recognition: open the Settings app, go to Apps › Echo Quillo, and turn on Speech Recognition.

Recording or the camera doesn't work.

Open the Settings app, go to Apps › Echo Quillo, and turn on Microphone or Camera. On a Mac, use System Settings › Privacy & Security.

My pronunciation isn't scored.

Pronunciation scoring runs on your device when your language is supported there. For other languages, you can turn on "Use Apple's speech service when needed" in Echo Quillo's Settings. It is off by default. When it is on, those recordings are sent to Apple for recognition.

The text from my photo has mistakes.

Crop the photo to just the text you want, and use a sharp, well-lit picture. You can fix any wrongly recognized words before you start practicing.

How do I use the same practices on my iPhone and Mac?

In Echo Quillo's Settings, choose a folder in iCloud Drive on each device. Echo Quillo keeps your speech, recordings and practice progress in an "EchoQuillo" folder there, so both devices see the same files.

Where is my data, and how do I delete it?

Everything is stored on your device, or in the folder you chose, such as iCloud Drive. There is no account. To delete your data, delete the app, and if you chose a folder, delete the "EchoQuillo" folder inside it. Read the privacy policy.
