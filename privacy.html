Privacy Policy for Echo Quillo

Last updated: October 25, 2026

What we collect

Echo Quillo does not collect personal information. We do not run servers that receive your data, we have no accounts, and we do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.

What stays on your device

Your practice material, recordings, progress and settings are stored on your device, or in the folder you choose, such as iCloud Drive. If you use iCloud Drive, Apple stores those files under your Apple account.

On-device processing

Text generation, translation, audio transcription and, for supported languages, pronunciation scoring are processed on your device.

Apple's speech service (optional)

For some languages, pronunciation scoring can't run on your device. Only if you turn on "Use Apple's speech service when needed" in Settings, the audio of your practice recording is sent to Apple for speech recognition. Apple handles this data under its own privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy). This setting is off by default. When it is off, your recordings are never sent anywhere, and languages that need the service can't be scored.

Permissions

Echo Quillo asks for access to the camera (to photograph text), the microphone (to record you) and speech recognition (to score and transcribe). You can change these in Settings at any time.

Children

Echo Quillo does not knowingly collect data from anyone.
